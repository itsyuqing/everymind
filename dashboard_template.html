Limitations. “Before” ratings are retrospective (asked after the training), which tends to overstate gains. The survey is anonymous and voluntary, so it reflects people who chose to respond. Most responses come from Anne Arundel and Prince George’s students; results for other counties, adults, and Spanish-language respondents rest on small samples. “Likely to call 988” was only asked on some survey versions. Comment themes are keyword-based and miss some meaning; read the comments themselves before acting on a small theme.
